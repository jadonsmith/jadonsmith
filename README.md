## 🎮 Games

Small browser games, all playable at **[play.jadon.me](https://play.jadon.me)**.

**Multiplayer**

- **[Polarity](https://github.com/jadonsmith/polarity)**: charge-flipping knockout on shrinking ice. Opposites attract, likes repel. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/polarity/)
- **[Gravity Wells](https://github.com/jadonsmith/gravity-wells)**: simultaneous-turn gravity artillery around stars and black holes. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/gravity/)
- **[Swirl](https://github.com/jadonsmith/swirl)**: fluid-dynamics territory game. Pour ink, push the liquid, spin vortices. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/swirl/)
- **[Sensory Overload](https://github.com/jadonsmith/sensory-overload)**: party game of three-second phone-sensor microgames: blow, shake, flip, spin, freeze. Slowest phone loses a heart. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/overload/)
- **[Hot Potato](https://github.com/jadonsmith/hot-potato)**: one phone passed round the room on a hidden fuse. Do the sensor dare (blow, shake, flip, spin…) to pass it on, and don't be holding it when it blows. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/potato/)
- **[Puff Regatta](https://github.com/jadonsmith/puff-regatta)**: blow-to-sail racing for 1–8 phones. Blow into the mic to fill your sail, tilt to steer, and race round the buoys until everyone's out of breath. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/regatta/)
- **[Brim](https://github.com/jadonsmith/brim)**: phone-sensor party relay. Your phone is a glass filled to the brim: run it round the furniture and pour it into your teammate's phone while the other team throws ice cubes at you. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/brim/)
- **[Chroma Hunt](https://github.com/jadonsmith/chroma-hunt)**: camera party game. Everyone gets the same colour and races to find something real that matches it. One snap each, scored on how close the colours look, and the photos go up side by side. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/chroma/)
- **[Wand Duel](https://github.com/jadonsmith/wand-duel)**: 1v1 phone duel. Trace glyphs in the air with your phone, counter what your rival throws, rally it back harder, and shout the spell's name for extra power. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/wand/)
- **[Parachute](https://github.com/jadonsmith/parachute)**: co-op party game for a big screen and phones. The TV shows a playground parachute and every phone is a handle: tilt to lift your edge, flick to send a wave, and launch balls through hoops together. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/parachute/)
- **[Tough Crowd](https://github.com/jadonsmith/tough-crowd)**: comedy-club party game for 3–10 phones. Write the funniest answer or play a card from your hand, win head-to-head duels, win over the judge in the Hot Seat, and land the Closer. Optional TV big screen. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/crowd/)
- **[Quick-Draw Duel](https://github.com/jadonsmith/quickdraw-duel)**: two-player drawing duel with matchmaking and private rooms. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/quickdraw/)
- **[Trivia Duel](https://github.com/jadonsmith/trivia-duel)**: two-player online trivia, powered by Open Trivia DB. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/trivia/)
- **[Evolve](https://github.com/jadonsmith/evolve)**: Slither/Tron-style multiplayer arena with bots. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/evolve/)

**Solo**

- **[Fractal Frontier](https://github.com/jadonsmith/fractal-frontier)**: WebGL2 Mandelbrot/Julia roguelike. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/fractal/)
- **[Back to Back](https://github.com/jadonsmith/back-to-back)**: 360° phone AR defence. Monsters come from every side, so turn around to zap them, solo or back to back with a friend. [▶ Play](https://play.jadon.me/backtoback/)
